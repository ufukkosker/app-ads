🔒 Vectura Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

Overview

Vectura ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our iOS puzzle game application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identifiable information directly. We utilize trusted third-party services that may collect the following types of data:

  • Device Identifiers: Used for advertising (IDFA / identifier for advertising, if permitted) and analytics.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous gameplay interactions, level progress metrics, and crash diagnostics.
  • Purchase History: Subscription status and transaction validation information for Vectura Pro subscriptions.
  • Advertising Data: Information concerning advertisements displayed and user interactions with them.

Third-Party Services

We work with the following industry-standard third-party service providers:

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements to free tier players. For more details, refer to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages in-app subscriptions and purchase receipts securely.
  • Apple StoreKit: Handles all financial transactions and payment processing directly on Apple's secure infrastructure.

Gameplay & Local Data Storage

All your puzzle progress, unlocked chapters, game board states, hints, and local settings are stored directly on your iOS device. We do not maintain user accounts, passwords, or personal profiles on external servers.

Children's Privacy

Our app is rated 4+ on the Apple App Store and contains content suitable for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your privacy and subscription choices:

  • You can disable personalized advertising at any time via your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can manage or cancel your auto-renewable Vectura Pro subscription at any time via your Apple ID Subscriptions settings.
  • You can reset or delete all locally stored game progress by uninstalling the application from your device.
